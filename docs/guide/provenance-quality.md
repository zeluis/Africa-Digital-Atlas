# Provenance, Quality Audits & Verification

To support academic credibility and transparency, the Atlas includes a Provenance & Data Quality console.

## What it does

- **Automated integrity audits** — verifies all 54 sovereign entities against the indicator store, detecting statistical anomalies, missing values, or out-of-bound outliers, in real time.
- **Cryptographic hash verification** — computes deterministic SHA-256 integrity hashes for indicator subsets, so data records can be verified as untampered.
- **Quality flags & footnotes** — flags observations that rely on modeled estimates, imputation, or lagged census reporting.
- **Academic citation generator** — generates formatted citations (APA, Harvard, Chicago, BibTeX) for any dataset or national profile.
