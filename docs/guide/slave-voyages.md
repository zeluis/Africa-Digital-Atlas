# Transatlantic Slave Voyages 3D Globe

A dedicated historical research subsystem visualizing the transatlantic slave trade, using data derived from the *Slave Voyages* research consortium.

## Features

- **Interactive 3D orthographic globe** — rotate, pitch, and inspect maritime trajectories connecting African coastal embarkation zones (Bight of Benin, Gold Coast, Senegambia, West Central Africa) to destination ports in Brazil, the Caribbean, and North America.
- **Animated great-circle flow arcs** — particle-animated bezier vectors whose stroke width and velocity represent total documented captive volumes over a selected period.
- **Spatiotemporal timeline scrubber** — filter across four centuries (1501–1600, 1601–1700, 1701–1800, 1801–1867) to observe the rise and decline of different imperial flag carriers (Portugal/Brazil, Great Britain, France, Netherlands, Spain, USA).
- **Academic export workbench** — download raw voyage data as CSV, or full literature citations in BibTeX.

Data covers over 36,000 documented voyages. See [Citations & Licensing](../data/citations-licensing.md) for attribution terms.
