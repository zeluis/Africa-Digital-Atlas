# Citations & Licensing

## Code license

The Africa Digital Atlas source code is released under the **MIT License**.

## Data licensing

Underlying statistical indicators remain subject to the licenses of their originating institutions:

- World Bank, IMF, WHO, UNESCO, UN Comtrade, UNDP, UNHCR, ILO, FAO, and ITU data are published under their respective open-data terms (most are CC BY 4.0 or equivalent).
- Slave Voyages Database records are provided by the Slave Voyages research consortium (Emory University et al.) — see their site for the specific citation format required for academic use.
- UNESCO World Heritage Site listings are sourced from the UNESCO World Heritage Centre.

## Citing the Atlas

If you use the Africa Digital Atlas in academic or research work, please cite both the platform and the specific underlying data source(s) you relied on. The in-app **Provenance & Data Quality** console (see the [User Guide](../guide/provenance-quality.md)) includes an academic citation generator that produces APA, Harvard, Chicago, and BibTeX formats for any dataset or country profile.
