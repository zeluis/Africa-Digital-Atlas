# Comparative Analytics

Tools for policy analysts and economists to quantitatively compare countries and indicators.

## Tools

* **Bi-variable correlation scatter plots** — plot any two indicators against each other (e.g. GDP per capita vs. female secondary education enrollment). The engine calculates the Pearson correlation coefficient (r), a trendline slope, and flags regional clusters and outliers.
* **Multi-country radar comparison** — select up to 4 countries and render a normalized radar chart across six pillars: Economic Output, Governance & Rule of Law, Healthcare Access, Digital Infrastructure, Education Equity, and Climate Resilience.
* **Rankings & leaderboards** — sortable, with ascending/descending order, subregional filtering, and distribution histograms.
