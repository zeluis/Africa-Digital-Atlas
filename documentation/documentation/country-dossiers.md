# Country Dossiers

Selecting any country on the map opens an in-depth dossier of verified structural data.

## What's inside

* **Geographic & political metadata** — ISO-3166-1 alpha-3 code, capital coordinates, official/national languages, currency and ISO-4217 code, land area, sovereign status, and UN Geoscheme classification.
* **Longitudinal trajectories** — multi-decade sparklines and time-series charts for GDP growth, fertility trends, electrification progress, and life expectancy.
* **Linguistic architecture** — national languages mapped to primary African language phyla (Niger-Congo, Afroasiatic, Nilo-Saharan, Khoisan, Austronesian).
* **UNESCO World Heritage Sites** — an integrated inventory of cultural, natural, and mixed sites for each country.
