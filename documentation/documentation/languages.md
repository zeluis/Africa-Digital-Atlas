# Languages

The platform includes an embedded, zero-dependency multilingual engine (`src/i18n.ts`).

## Supported languages

* **English (EN)** — default
* **French (FR)** — official administrative language across West & Central Africa
* **Arabic (AR)** — official across North Africa & the Horn of Africa, with dynamic right-to-left (RTL) layout switching
* **Portuguese (PT)** — official for PALOP nations (Angola, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau, Cabo Verde, São Tomé)
* **Swahili (SW)** — major regional trade language of the East African Community
* **Spanish (ES)** — official language of Equatorial Guinea
* **German (DE)** — standard international research locale

Number, currency, and date formatting adapts automatically to the chosen language (e.g. decimal separators, Latin vs. Arabic numerals).
