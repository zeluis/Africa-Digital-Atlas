# Vector Cartography Map

The core cartographic viewport renders pure SVG path geometries calibrated specifically for the African continental landmass and surrounding island archipelagos — no raster map tiles.

## What you can do

* **Choropleth coloring** — dynamically scale colors across any selected indicator (GDP per capita, HDI, renewable energy share, fragility index, etc.) using quantile or linear interpolation palettes.
* **Micro-state locators** — Cabo Verde, São Tomé and Príncipe, Comoros, Mauritius, and Seychelles get dedicated high-contrast callout rings with enlarged click targets (44px minimum) for touchscreens.
* **Viewport controls** — smooth zoom, drag-to-pan, and one-click subregional focus presets (All Africa, North Africa, West Africa, Central Africa, East Africa, Southern Africa).
* **Interactive tooltips** — hover any country for a floating card with its name, capital, primary indicator value, regional ranking, and a data-quality badge.

See [Provenance & Data Quality](provenance-and-data-quality.md) for what the quality badges mean, and [Data & Methodology](https://fpizeluis.gitbook.io/fpizeluis-docs/api-reference/) for where the indicator values come from.
