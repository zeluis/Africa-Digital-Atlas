# Thematic Pillars

Indicators and encyclopedic dossiers across the Atlas are organized into **8 thematic pillars**, used throughout the Thematic Pillars portal, country dossiers, and comparative analytics:

1. **History & Deep Time** — Pre-colonial states, archaeological heritage, and historical trajectories.
2. **Demographics & Peoples** — Population structure, urbanization, and linguistic/cultural heritage.
3. **Macroeconomy & Trade** — GDP trajectories, debt sustainability, bilateral trade flows, and AfCFTA integration.
4. **Society, Health & Education** — Healthcare access, literacy, education equity, and social protection.
5. **Climate, Ecology & Energy** — Climate vulnerability, ecological biomes, and renewable energy transition.
6. **Governance & Geopolitics** — Rule of law, institutional capacity, peace & security, and regional blocs.
7. **Infrastructure & Corridors** — Transport networks, maritime ports, digital connectivity, and power grids.
8. **Tourism & Cultural Heritage** — UNESCO World Heritage sites, conservation reserves, and cultural landmarks.

Each pillar aggregates underlying indicators and documented references (see [Data & Methodology](https://fpizeluis.gitbook.io/fpizeluis-docs/api-reference/) for the full catalog) for cross-country comparison across all 54 sovereign African states.
